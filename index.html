COMPETITION FORMAT & ROUNDS

The event features four houses, each with two teams of two participants, competing across four progressively challenging rounds.

Round 1: Tech IQ

Difficulty: Easy

Format: Participants will receive a technology-related question with four multiple-choice options.

Focus: Core technology concepts, basic terminology, and general facts.

Round 2: Fact or Fiction

Difficulty: Mild

Format: The host will read a popular tech rumor or statement. Participants must determine if it's True or False.

Focus: Separating popular myth from reality, recent product leaks, and market trends.

Round 3: The Buzzer Blitz

Difficulty: Hard

Format: The host will read an advanced question. The fastest participant to press their buzzer gets to answer.

Focus: In-depth knowledge, technical specifics, and quick recall under pressure.

Round 4: The Bonus Lens

Difficulty: Variable (Bonus Points)

Format: An image, video, or audio clip will be shown. Participants must answer a question related to the media.

Focus: Recognizing key tech personalities, products, and events through multimedia.
